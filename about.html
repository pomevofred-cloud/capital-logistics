/ Our story

A logistics partner for the DRC's next chapter

Capital Logistics SARL was founded on a simple observation: the DRC's economic potential is bottlenecked by the state of its logistics infrastructure. Ports move slower than they should. Corridors run at fractions of their capacity.

What began as focused container haulage on the Matadi–Kinshasa trunk grew corridor by corridor into a nine-route network, Atlantic ports, capital hub, inland cities. Fleet. Bonded warehousing. Customs expertise.

Today, Capital Logistics is a leading transport and logistics enterprise in the DRC. Clients trust us because we understand the terrain, and because we're building what the country's economy needs next.
